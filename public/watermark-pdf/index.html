Flisk Studio

Put a watermark on every page of a PDF

Stamp text such as Confidential or Draft across every page. It runs on your own computer, so your document is never uploaded.

Open the Studio

How it works

  1. Open the Studio and choose Watermark PDFs.
  2. Pick one or more PDFs.
  3. Type your text. Any language works, including Ukrainian, Dutch and German.
  4. Press Process, look at the result, and download.

Good to know

  • The text is drawn large, at an angle, and lightly, so the page stays readable.
  • Several PDFs at once come back as one ZIP.

Questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.

Can the watermark be removed?

It is drawn on top of the page, not a separate layer. Someone with PDF editing software may be able to cover it, so it deters casual copying; it is not security.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password first.

Is it free?

Yes, free for now.
