Flisk Studio

Make big PDFs small, in your browser

Scanned documents and photo-heavy PDFs can shrink a lot. Your files are processed on your own computer and never uploaded.

Open the Studio

How it works

  1. Open the Studio and choose Shrink PDFs.
  2. Pick your PDFs and set a quality (lower is smaller).
  3. Press Process, look at the result and the before and after sizes, and download.

Good to know

  • How it works: each page is redrawn as a picture. That removes hidden text and metadata, but the text can no longer be selected or searched.
  • PDFs that are mostly text are already small. Turning them into pictures would make them bigger, so the Studio keeps the original and tells you.
  • Scans and photo-heavy PDFs are where it helps. In one test a 2 MB three-page scan became about 0.9 MB; results vary with the file.

Questions

Why did my PDF not get smaller?

It is probably mostly text, which is already compact. The Studio keeps the original instead of making it bigger.

Can I still select the text after shrinking?

No. The pages become pictures. Keep the original if you need searchable text.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. Nothing leaves your computer.

Is it free?

Yes, free for now.
