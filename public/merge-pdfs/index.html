Flisk Studio

Merge PDFs into one file, in your browser

Join several PDF files into one, in the order you choose. Your documents never leave your computer, which matters for contracts, invoices and IDs.

Open the Studio

How it works

  1. Open the Studio and choose Merge PDFs.
  2. Pick your PDFs. Each shows a picture of page one and its page count.
  3. Use the up and down arrows to set the order. The list order is the merge order.
  4. Press Process, look at the result, and download merged.pdf.

Good to know

  • Add more files at any time: picking again adds to the list.
  • Remove a file with the cross next to it.

Questions

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. The merge happens in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password first, then merge.

Is there a page limit?

Only what your computer can handle.

Is it free?

Yes, free for now, with no account.
